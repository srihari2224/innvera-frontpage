Hero Background Video (Most Important Asset)
▶
Video Asset
This is the single most important asset on the entire site. The INNVERA text animates first, then as the visitor scrolls, this video reveals through a cinematic parallelogram wipe. Every college admin who visits will watch this first. It must feel premium, modern, and instantly communicate what PrintIT does.
What the video must show — shot list
The video is a 15–25 second seamless loop. It plays muted, autoplays, and loops silently behind the INNVERA hero text. The mood must be dark, cinematic, high-contrast — exactly like a product launch film. Think iPhone launch video aesthetic but for a printing kiosk.
🎥
intro.mp4 — Hero loop video
/public/assets/videos/intro.mp4
Videographer Brief — give this exactly to your cinematographer
Shoot a cinematic 20-second seamlessly looping product video for PrintIT, a self-service printing kiosk for college campuses. The video will play muted behind large white text on a black website.

Shot sequence (cut together, total 20–25 seconds):

Shot 1 (4–5 sec): Extreme close-up of a student's hand unlocking their phone. The screen glows white in a dark corridor. The glow should fill the frame from the right side. Shallow depth of field — the hand is sharp, everything behind is dark and blurred.

Shot 2 (3–4 sec): Screen recording / phone mockup of the PrintIT app — someone selecting print settings (B&W toggle, number of copies). This should be shot as a screen capture or a close-up of the phone screen with the app UI visible.

Shot 3 (4–5 sec): Medium shot: student walks up to the PrintIT kiosk in a dark corridor. The kiosk screen glows coral/orange. Student taps the screen. Backlit from behind — the kiosk light creates a rim light on the student's silhouette.

Shot 4 (3–4 sec): Extreme close-up: papers emerge from the printer slot, soft motion blur. The white of the paper against the dark kiosk body is the hero moment of the video.

Shot 5 (4–5 sec): Wide shot of a campus corridor or library at night, the kiosk glowing in the middle distance. Students pass by or study nearby. The kiosk is a beacon of light in an otherwise dark space.

Technical specs: Shoot in 4K, export at 1920×1080 H.264 under 8MB. Grade dark — lift the blacks only slightly, push contrast. Color temperature: cool/blue-tinted highlights, warm kiosk screen. NO camera shake — use gimbal or tripod. The video loops: last frame must match first frame visually (both show the dark corridor or kiosk glow).
AI Video Prompt (Sora / Runway / Kling)
Prompt: Cinematic dark short film, self-service printing kiosk glowing with warm orange light in a dark university corridor at night. A student approaches, their silhouette backlit by the kiosk screen. They tap the touchscreen. Papers emerge from a slot in slow motion, crisp white against dark background. Camera tracks smoothly from medium shot to close-up on the paper output. Moody blue-black ambient light, single warm point-of-light from kiosk. Photorealistic. 24fps film grain. No text. No logos.

Style reference: Apple product launch video, Dyson product film, dark editorial cinematography.
Negative prompt: cartoon, animation, bright daylight, people smiling at camera, stock footage feel, text overlays, logos.
Format: .mp4 H.264 · 1920×1080 · under 8MB · seamless loop · no audio

about1.jpg — Student uploading at phone
/public/assets/images/about1.jpg
Photography Direction
Subject: A college student (18–22 years old) standing in a dimly lit campus space — a corridor, stairwell, or library corner. They're looking down at their smartphone. The phone screen casts a soft upward glow on their face.

Composition: Shot from slightly below eye-level, portrait orientation. Student takes up left 2/3 of frame. Right side shows the dark background. Student should not be looking at camera — they're focused on their phone.

Lighting: Predominantly dark environment. Primary light source is the phone screen — cool white glow. Optional: a single practical lamp or campus light in the background creating depth.

Mood: Modern, focused, self-reliant. This student doesn't need help — they're solving a problem themselves.

Technical: Shot at f/1.8–2.2, ISO 800–1600 for natural grain. No flash. Slight underexposure — keep the darks dark.

kiosk-hero.jpg — Clean product shot, studio style
Photography Direction — set up this shot yourself
Setup: Place the kiosk against a pure black backdrop (black fabric or a dark wall in a dark room). Use two lights: one key light from 45° left (creates the primary shape and highlights the kiosk edges), one rim light from behind-right (separates the kiosk from the background with a bright edge). No fill light — keep shadows deep.

Shot: 3/4 angle view of the full kiosk — the screen should be ON and showing the PrintIT UI (app home screen). The printer slot and output tray should be visible. Eye-level height. Kiosk centered in frame with breathing room on all sides.

The screen matters: Show the actual PrintIT app home screen on the kiosk display. Use a long exposure or composite if needed to make the screen visible without blowout.

Result: A crisp, dramatic product shot that could appear in a tech magazine. This is your most reusable marketing asset.

logo-mark.svg — Abstract kiosk/I mark
Design Brief for a designer or AI (Figma / Adobe Illustrator)
Design a minimal geometric logomark for Innvera Technology / PrintIT. The mark should work at 28×28px and at 200×200px equally well.

Concept direction A (Recommended): An abstract letterform "I" that is composed of two overlapping rectangles at a slight angle — evoking both the letter I and a printed sheet of paper sliding out of a machine. Monochrome. Works in white on black (website) and black on white (proposals).

Concept direction B: A minimal kiosk silhouette — a tall rectangle with a smaller screen rectangle and a horizontal slot line at the bottom. Reduced to 3–4 geometric shapes total.

Style rules: No gradients. No rounded corners (matches the site's 0px border-radius). Pure geometric. Weight similar to a medium-bold font stroke. Clean enough to embroider on a polo shirt.
SVG vector · 28×28 artboard · single path or 2–3 shapes max · monochrome
