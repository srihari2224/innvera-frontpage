Hero — Full Screen Opening
▶
Asset-heavy section
The scroll animation (parallelogram reveal), INNVERA text animation, and the overall structure are all excellent — keep everything. Only replace the video and the small handwritten tagline SVG at the bottom.
Video: /public/assets/videos/intro.mp4
🎥
Hero background video — most important asset
Film a 15–30 second looping video of someone using the PrintIT kiosk: person walks up, takes out phone, uploads a document, the kiosk prints, they collect papers and walk away. Shoot at night or in a dark corridor — this makes the black background of the hero look intentional and cinematic. No audio needed (muted autoplay). If you don't have the kiosk yet, shoot a person using a touchscreen in a dark room — any screen works as a placeholder.
Format: .mp4 (H.264) · Dimensions: 1920×1080 or 1080×1920 (portrait also works with object-cover) · File size: under 8MB · Loop seamlessly
🖼
Alternative if no video yet: dark abstract background
Replace the <video> tag with a Next.js <Image> component. Use a high-contrast dark image: a close-up of a circuit board, glowing printer nozzle, or dark campus corridor at night. The parallelogram reveal animation still looks stunning with a static image.
Format: .jpg · Dimensions: 1920×1080 · Compressed to under 300KB
Handwritten tagline SVG (bottom of hero)
